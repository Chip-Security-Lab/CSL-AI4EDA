# Side-channel example evidence scope

The supplied maintainer notes report partial improvements on selected synthetic, condition-specific type examples. Those records do not establish success on arbitrary RTL, across models, or on every rerun. No new online experiment was performed during publication preparation.

The representative inputs and resource declarations are preserved in [examples/sidechannel-online](examples/sidechannel-online/README.md). Use actual task evidence when reproducing a case. A formal result that remains inconclusive must not be described as proved; RTL mitigation is not physical security.

The original detailed historical notes remain inside the unchanged VSIX. The [type matrix](SIDECHANNEL_TYPES.md) and [result contract](ACCEPTANCE_CONTRACT.md) give the public support boundary.
